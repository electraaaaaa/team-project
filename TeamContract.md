## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use a Discord server as our primary communication channel.

* Team members will respond to messages within 24 hours on weekdays.

* Teammates should notify in the server if they think they won't be able to meet a deadline at least 1 day before, if they have to miss lecture, or other important subjects.

* Make sure to actively listen to each other, give everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute, etc.

* Meet up on Fridays or Weekends in person, otherwise set up a call on Discord.

## Decision Making

* How will decisions about your team project be made? By majority vote.

---
## Conflict resolution

* How will your team resolve conflicts?
  1. The team will first attempt to resolve the issue privately through open discussion - list out your reasons etc.
  2. If issue persists, the team will reach out to a course instructor/TA for mediation.

---

## Accountability

* Reliability and accountability are also important aspects of teamwork. What are the responsibilities of each team member? (Example: completing their share of the work in a timely manner, seeking assistance from teammates/TAs/instructors when required, etc.)
  - Have a good division of responsibilities
  - Do your work on time
  - Check discord regularly
  - We'll have soft and hard deadlines. Try to finish your work by the soft deadline, but in the worst case, finish by the hard deadline.
